AI plugins for Jira that stay under your control

Prompts and task trees, directly in your issues.

One workflow, two Jira plugins

Simple AI Workflow has two apps you can use together or separately. Both keep everything inside Jira and always let humans approve changes first.

Plugin 01

Simple AI Workflow Prompts

A shared library of prompts in Jira, so everyone uses the same, tested wording.

  • Central prompt catalog per team or project.
  • One‑click use in issue descriptions or comments.
  • Works with your chosen AI provider.

Plugin 02

Simple AI Workflow Tasks

Turn a short description of a feature into a proposed set of epics, stories, and tasks.

  • Explain once; get a structured task tree.
  • Edit and approve before anything is created.
  • Fits into your existing Jira boards.
Install How it works

Prompts: consistent AI, less copy‑paste

Instead of scattered prompts in chats, your team uses a shared catalog inside Jira. You control which prompts exist and how they are updated.

// Example: refine a bug ticket
Prompt: "Rewrite this issue so it is ready for development."

// In Jira:
1. Pick "Bug: ready for dev".
2. The plugin sends the issue context to your AI provider.
3. Summary, description and ACs come back cleaned up.

Tasks: fast work breakdown

For bigger changes, describing the work is easy but turning it into tickets is slow. Tasks proposes a structure you can accept, tweak, or discard.

// Example: usage‑based billing
Input: "Implement usage‑based billing with Stripe."

// The plugin proposes:
- Epic: "Usage‑based billing integration"
- Stories and tasks under it, ready for your review.

How Simple AI Workflow Prompts works

1

Select your AI

Choose your preferred AI tool from the dropdown menu. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any custom endpoint.

2

Choose what to include

Select the data points you need (summary, comments, linked issues) and your language.

3

Generate & copy

Click once to generate a formatted prompt, then copy it to your AI tool.

How Simple AI Workflow Tasks works

1

Open the Tasks view

In your Jira project, open the Simple AI Workflow Tasks view to start the breakdown flow.

2

Describe the work

Paste or type what you want to build. The plugin prepares it for AI to generate a task tree.

3

Review & create issues

Review the proposed epics, stories and tasks, adjust them, then create them in Jira.

Try Simple AI Workflow or customise it
