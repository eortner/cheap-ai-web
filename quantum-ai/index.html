The project

A new kind of development environment

Quantum AI IDE is an AWS-based environment tailored specifically for quantum coding. Rather than adapting classical IDEs to quantum workflows, we built around the unique demands of quantum programming — from circuit design to hybrid classical‑quantum execution — with AI assistance woven in at every step.

Built on AWS Braket

Native integration with AWS Braket for circuit simulation and execution on real quantum hardware.

AI-assisted quantum coding

Intelligent suggestions and code generation tailored to quantum programming patterns and gate operations.

Quantum-first design

Every feature is designed around how quantum workflows actually operate, not retrofitted from classical tooling.
