Service Level Agreement (SLA) Statement

Last Updated: March 1, 2026

1. Service Availability

Simple Workflow AI Creator & Simple Workflow AI Prompts are plugins that operate within your Atlassian Jira instance. Our service commitment covers:

Plugin Uptime: 99.5% Monthly

We guarantee that the plugin's core functionality will be available 99.5% of the time, excluding scheduled maintenance and Atlassian platform outages.

AI Prompt Generation: 99.9% Success Rate

Successful generation of AI prompts and workflows from Jira issue data for both Creator and Prompts plugins, excluding cases where Jira API is unavailable or malformed issue data.

Measurement: Uptime is calculated monthly based on error rate and usage analytics via Atlassian Forge monitoring.

3. Maintenance

Scheduled Maintenance

We perform scheduled maintenance during Atlassian's standard maintenance windows. Advance notice of 5 business days will be provided for any maintenance requiring plugin updates.

Updates

Critical security updates will be prioritised and submitted to the Atlassian Marketplace as soon as possible following identification. Release timing is subject to Atlassian's app review and approval process, which is outside our direct control. Feature updates are released on a monthly cadence.

4. Exclusions

The SLA does not cover failures due to:

  • Atlassian Jira platform outages or maintenance
  • Customer's network or infrastructure issues
  • Third-party plugins conflicting with Simple Workflow AI Creator & Simple Workflow AI Prompts
  • Unauthorized modifications to the plugin code
  • Issues caused by malformed Jira issue data
  • Force majeure events

5. Service Credits

As this app is distributed via the Atlassian Marketplace under the "Paid via Atlassian" model, all billing and payment processing is managed exclusively by Atlassian. In the event of a service disruption attributable to this plugin, affected users may contact us at contactcheapai@gmail.com to report the incident. We will investigate and work with Atlassian's billing team where applicable to seek appropriate remediation. We do not directly issue monetary credits or refunds.

6. SLA Monitoring & Reporting

We continuously monitor performance across both Simple Workflow AI Creator and Prompts plugins via error rate and usage analytics provided by Atlassian Forge monitoring. Upon request, we can provide uptime reports for your instance.

7. Changes to SLA

This SLA may be updated with 30 days' notice. Changes will be posted on this page and notified via email to registered users.
