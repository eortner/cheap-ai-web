Simple AI Workflow has two apps you can use together or separately. Both keep everything inside Jira and always let humans approve changes first.

Prompts: consistent AI, less copy‑paste

Instead of scattered prompts in chats, your team uses a shared catalog inside Jira. You control which prompts exist and how they are updated.

1

Open Simple Workflow AI panel

Launch the plugin directly inside your Jira issue — no context switching.

↓
2

Choose language and tone

Select the language and tone that fits your team or the specific ticket.

↓
3

Generate prompt for your AI provider

Get a ready-to-use prompt built from your issue context — copy it straight to ChatGPT, Claude, or any other tool.

Tasks: fast work breakdown

For bigger changes, describing the work is easy but turning it into tickets is slow. Tasks bridges your AI conversation and your Jira board.

1

Describe the feature in plain language with your AI

Have a natural conversation with your preferred AI provider about what needs to be built.

↓
2

Copy our prompt to your AI to generate the tasks

Use the plugin's structured prompt to instruct your AI to output a proper epic, story and task tree.

↓
3

Parse and create the issues in Jira

The plugin reads the AI output, lets you review the structure, then creates all issues directly in your board.

Tests: regression coverage from your issues

Stop writing test tickets by hand. Point the plugin at any issue and get a structured regression suite ready to track — all inside Jira.

1

Open the issue and generate a test prompt

The plugin reads your issue context and builds a ready-to-use prompt for your AI provider.

↓
2

Paste to your AI — get back structured JSON

Your AI returns a set of regression tests in a structured JSON format the plugin understands.

↓
3

Parse, create sub-issues and track

The plugin creates each test as a sub-issue linked to the original, then lets you track pass/fail status directly on a built-in board.
